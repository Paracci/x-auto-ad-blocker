How it works

Smart. Silent. Surgical.

The extension works with X's visible interface and user-triggered media requests. No filter lists to maintain and no background telemetry.

About
Built for a cleaner feed

Detect sponsored posts, choose the action, and download media without handing your settings to a server.

How it works
  • Detects ad labels with a debounced observer
  • Hides or blocks according to your setting
  • Applies personal filters locally
  • Downloads media when you ask
Privacy

No third-party analytics or telemetry. Settings and history stay in chrome.storage.local.

01

Detects in real time

A debounced MutationObserver watches for new tweets and checks exact ad labels outside the tweet body.

02

Respects the selected action

Use Block account on X for a lasting account-level action, or Hide post only for a local and reversible choice.

03

Keeps local history useful

Unique advertiser handles, categories, counters, filters, and blocked history are stored locally and rendered in the popup.

04

Downloads only on click

The background worker resolves tweet media through X's public syndication endpoint only after you press the download button, then fetches the selected file.

05

Handles X's dynamic UI

Hydration-aware retries, temporary interaction shielding, and rescan-on-reenable keep the experience stable as the feed changes.

Privacy

Zero telemetry.
Clear boundaries.

The extension is designed to keep your controls and history on your device while being precise about the X requests needed for media downloads.

No third-party telemetry

No analytics, tracking pixels, crash reporting, or settings transmission. The extension does not send your X activity to a third party.

Only when you download

When you click download, the extension uses X's public syndication endpoint to resolve the best file, then fetches the selected media from X's media hosts. Nothing is sent to a third party.

Local storage only

Settings, filters, counters, language preference, and blocked history live in chrome.storage.local on your device.

Installation

Up in a minute.

The extension is distributed from GitHub and installed directly in Chrome Developer Mode.

Install in Developer Mode

  1. 1

    Clone or download this repository as a ZIP.

  2. 2

    Open chrome://extensions/ in Chrome.

  3. 3

    Turn on Developer mode.

  4. 4

    Select Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder.

  5. 5

    Open x.com, refresh, and browse with your chosen settings.
